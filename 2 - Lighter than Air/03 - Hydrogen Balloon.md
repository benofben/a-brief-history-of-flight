# Hydrogen Balloon

This is a page in a children's book.  The aspect ratio should be 1:1.  There should be an image with the text overlaid.

## Text

Just ten days after Montgolfier, French physicist Jacques Charles and engineer Nicolas-Louis Robert flew to a height of 25,000 feet in a rubberized balloon filled with hydrogen.

Their flight took them 25 miles.  It lasted over 2 hours.  It was the longest, higest and furthest flight to that point in history.
